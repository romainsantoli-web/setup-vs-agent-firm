## 0. Market context (frames all other questions)

### Takeaway
The mobile games market is mature and flat-to-declining: revenue is held up by spending from existing players (live-ops), while downloads keep falling. A new entrant can't count on a growing market. It has to take players from someone else or reach segments and channels the incumbents serve badly.

### Cited Findings
- Sensor Tower *State of Gaming 2026* (published 26 Feb 2026): 52 B game downloads in 2025, 50 B of them on mobile. Google Play had 81% of game downloads and the App Store 15% — [Sensor Tower press release](https://sensortower.com/press/press-release-sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-gaming-drove-52-billion-downloads-82b-iap-revenue-on-mobile-and-12b-premium-revenue-on-steam); [full PDF](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/2026-02-26-sensor_tower__state_of_gaming_2026__en_wp.pdf)
- Mobile IAP revenue in 2025 was $82 B, up about 1% YoY. Revenue per download rose to $1.62. App Store game downloads fell 5.7% YoY (7.8 B) and Google Play fell 7.3% YoY (42.4 B) — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/press/press-release-sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-gaming-drove-52-billion-downloads-82b-iap-revenue-on-mobile-and-12b-premium-revenue-on-steam); [Respawn/Outlook India summary](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/mobile-games-82-billion-revenue-2025-sensor-tower-report)
- 1% growth is described as the lowest mobile growth rate in five years. The same piece names *R.E.P.O.* and *Peak*, both PC games, as the breakout indies, and says the top mobile 4X earners are Chinese-made *Last War: Survival* and *Whiteout Survival* — [dev.to analysis (secondary)](https://dev.to/linou518/2026-game-market-mobile-is-maturing-pc-indie-games-are-the-real-opportunity-4hkl)
- H1 2026: mobile game revenue about $40 B (−2% YoY) and downloads about 24 B (−12% YoY), per Sensor Tower-based figures — [Tech Insider (secondary; some monthly numbers there look garbled)](https://tech-insider.org/mobile-game-revenue-decline-2026/)
- August 2026: $6.57 B consumer spend and 3.76 B downloads across the App Store and Google Play — [shattered.io citing Sensor Tower](https://shattered.io/mobile-gaming-6-57-billion-roblox-downloads-2026/)
- Newzoo's broader 2025 mobile estimate of about $113 B includes third-party Android stores and off-store/web-shop spending. That explains the gap with Sensor Tower's $82 B — [shanethegamer research (secondary)](https://www.shanethegamer.com/research/mobile-gaming-facts/)

### Inferences
- Falling downloads mean user acquisition (UA) costs per install will stay high. That favours designs with built-in virality (social/party, shareable moments) and distribution outside the stores (web, YouTube, Discord, Telegram) over paid-UA-dependent F2P.
- Rising revenue per download alongside falling installs means incumbents are pushing monetisation harder on fewer players. That fits with the player fatigue documented in section 1.

- Could not get the Newzoo Global Games Market Report 2025/2026 directly. The $113 B figure is second-hand.
- No GameAnalytics or Liftoff 2025/2026 benchmark data turned up in searches (retention/CPI by genre is missing).

## 1. Underserved segments & needs (premium/no-ads, short sessions, local multiplayer, 35+, women, families, cozy, offline)

### Takeaway
The clearest documented pain point is intrusive ads and aggressive F2P monetisation. Players churn because of them, and a measurable premium/no-ads counter-trend appeared in 2025. In France the player base is adult (average age 40), close to gender parity and mostly casual on mobile. That is a large audience that hyper-aggressive F2P mid-core games serve poorly.

### Cited Findings
**Ads and monetisation fatigue**
- Bain Gamer Survey 2025: 64% of players say ads interrupt their gaming experience (+5 pts YoY). Yet 46% say they often make in-game purchases triggered by ads — [Bain & Company, 2025](https://www.bain.com/insights/gamer-survey-great-gameplay-is-no-longer-enough-gaming-report-2025/)
- Deloitte × Google AdMob (June 2025, 7,000 gamers): casual gamers are 30–50% more likely to churn after encountering disruptive ad features, and repeated exposure pushes 52% of all gamers to quit a game entirely — [Deloitte press release, 10 Jun 2025](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/about/press-room/deloitte-improve-mobile-game-advertising.html); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/growth-in-mobile-gaming-new-global-study-from-deloitte-and-google-admob-reveals-the-link-between-mobile-ad-engagement-and-gamer-retention-302477095.html)
- AppHarbr research: 45% delete a game because of overly frequent interstitials — [Plarium monetisation stats (aggregator)](https://plarium.com/en/blog/mobile-game-monetization-statistics/)
- Analysis of player reviews: players accept spending on cosmetics but resent paying for power (pay-to-win) — [AppFollow, 2026 monetisation playbook](https://appfollow.io/blog/mobile-game-monetization)
- Industry commentary on bad ads degrading mobile UX — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/bad-ads-are-killing-the-mobile-gaming-experience-heres-how-we-fix-it/)

**Premium / no-ads demand (evidence of a real gap)**
- Premium mobile releases rose 77% in 2025, to nearly 750 premium titles (AppMagic data). F2P still accounts for 96% of downloads. PC-to-mobile ports rose from 7 (2024) to 23 (2025). Insiders cite subscriptions (Apple Arcade, Netflix Games) and player fatigue with F2P monetisation as drivers — [GameDev.net news, 2026](https://gamedev.net/news/premium-mobile-games-are-back-with-releases-up-77-in-2025-r4367/); [daily.dev mirror](https://daily.dev/posts/premium-mobile-games-are-back-with-releases-up-77-in-2025-mo3xb5ehx)
- *Balatro* mobile ($9.99 upfront, no IAP, no ads): AppMagic estimates about $21.3 M in mobile revenue and more than 3.1 M downloads (per the same GameDev.net report). Earlier it was near $4.4 M by Dec 2024, boosted by 5 Game Awards nominations, and close to $1 M in its first 7 days on mobile — [PocketGamer.biz $4.4m](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/balatro-nears-44m-on-mobile-amid-a-sudden-spending-surge/); [PocketGamer.biz first week](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/balatro-approaches-1-million-in-seven-days-on-mobile/); [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/balatro-has-reportedly-made-over-4million-dollars-on-mobile-since-release/). Over 5 M sales across all platforms — [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/balatro/five-million-sales/). **Conflict:** Statista states over $9 M of "IAP revenue" by Jan 2025 — [Statista](https://statista.com/statistics/1546856/balatro-global-unit-sales). This likely counts the upfront purchase as store revenue, since the game has no IAP.
- Indie mobile award finalists for 2026 (GamingonPhone Awards) include SILT, Backpack Battles, Gambonanza, Lost Twins 2, TOEM, Sea of Stars and Kingdom Rush Battles. Many are premium or PC ports. These are critical picks, not sales data — [GamingonPhone Awards 2026](https://gamingonphone.com/awards/gpa-2026/indie-mobile-game-of-the-year/)

**France / Europe demographics (SELL × Médiamétrie)**
- 40.2 M French people played at least once in 2025, more than 7 in 10. 76% play at least weekly. Players use 2.2 devices on average, with smartphone (59%) and console (58%) the most cited — [SELL "Les Français et le jeu vidéo", Sept 2025](https://www.sell.fr/sites/default/files/essentiel-jeu-video/ejv_septembre_2025_def_0.pdf); [AFJV summary](https://afjv.com/news/11790_etude-mediametrie-sell-francais-jeux-video-2025.htm)
- Average player age is 40 and 88% of players are adults. The split is 51% men and 49% women. The survey ran online 19 Jun–13 Jul 2025 with 4,001 people aged 10–80 — [AFJV](https://afjv.com/news/11790_etude-mediametrie-sell-francais-jeux-video-2025.htm); [GamerGen](https://gamergen.com/actualites/l-essentiel-jeu-video-2025-comment-francais-jouent-ils-vraiment-2025-341101-1); [LaRevueD'Geek](https://www.larevuedgeek.fr/sell-2025-la-france-joue-plus-que-jamais-40-millions-de-joueuses-et-joueurs-connectes/)
- On smartphone, the top genres are casual (44%), strategy/RTS (15%) and social games (12%) — [SELL Sept 2025 via search summary](https://www.sell.fr/sites/default/files/essentiel-jeu-video/ejv_septembre_2025_def_0.pdf)
- The French video game market was €5.856 B in 2025 (+3%). Mobile was €1.792 B, 30% of the total and up 11% — [SELL Bilan du marché 2025, Apr 2026](https://www.sell.fr/sites/default/files/essentiel-jeu-video/ejv_avril_2026_final_.pdf); [Ultimate Gaming breakdown (secondary)](https://ultimate-gaming.fr/industrie/marche-jeu-video-france-2026/). Mobile is growing faster than the overall French market (+11% vs +3%), unlike the flat global IAP picture.
- Younger groups, especially 18–24, are over-represented in free mobile games — [Plarium citing Business of Apps](https://plarium.com/en/blog/mobile-game-monetization-statistics/)

### Inferences
- **Gap 1, "fair premium / no-ads" for adults:** large adult casual audiences (France: average age 40, 49% women, 44% casual on mobile) dislike ads (64% find them interruptive) but are mainly served by ad-heavy casual games. The success of *Balatro* and the 77% rise in premium releases suggest willingness to pay upfront for quality, ad-free, short-session games, especially when there is a PC/console halo or press/award exposure.
- **Gap 2, "short but meaningful" sessions:** roguelite-deckbuilders (*Balatro*, *Backpack Battles*, *Gambonanza*) fit 5–15 minute sessions with real depth. That is the opposite of the "time-gated grind" pattern players criticise.
- Monetisation without pay-to-win (cosmetics, one-time unlock, optional supporter pack) is the model most compatible with the documented sentiment.
- The data does not prove that families, couch co-op or offline play are underserved. Those are hypotheses (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 survey data found specifically on women 35+, cozy-game demand, families, offline-play demand or couch co-op on phones. Unity Gaming Report, Liftoff and Think with Google sources did not surface in searches.
- No reliable pay-to-win survey percentage found, only qualitative review analysis.
- Could not verify revenue for premium mobile ports other than *Balatro* (e.g., Vampire Survivors mobile went F2P with ads; no 2025–2026 data found).

## 2. Generative AI in games 2025–2026 (AI-native games, player sentiment, on-device AI)

### Takeaway
Player sentiment toward generative AI is strongly negative in the largest survey, especially among under-25s, and Steam data show a measurable review penalty for disclosed gen-AI use. As of 2026 no breakout AI-native mobile hit is confirmed outside China. On-device inference (Gemini Nano via AICore, Snapdragon Game AI SDK) removes per-player server cost but works only on recent flagships. AI is an opportunity only if it serves visible gameplay value (systemic play, adaptive game master) and is not perceived as cost-cutting.

### Cited Findings
**Player sentiment**
- Quantic Foundry (18 Dec 2025, nearly 2 M respondents): about 85% hold a negative attitude toward gen AI in games and about 63% (62.7%) chose the most negative option. Positive attitudes: 3% of ages 13–17, 7% of ages 18–24, 22% of ages 45+. Attitudes also vary by gender and gaming motivation — [Quantic Foundry](https://quanticfoundry.com/2025/12/18/gen-ai/) (via search summary; full page fetch failed)
- Steam review analysis of 508,192 English reviews: full-release games disclosing gen-AI had about a 65.7% recommendation rate versus 86.8% for procedural-generation games. Thematic coding of 600 reviews shows players read gen-AI use as a sign of low developer investment — [arXiv 2608.11539](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11539); [Pith Review](https://pith.science/paper/2608.11539). (Note: the arXiv ID points to Aug 2026. One summary listed a later date. Preprint, not peer reviewed.)
- Circana's Mat Piscatella: there is a vocal minority against gen AI, and most players don't currently care much either way — [shanethegamer AI in gaming stats (secondary)](https://www.shanethegamer.com/research/ai-in-gaming-statistics/). **Conflicts** with the Quantic Foundry figures. A smaller Steam-focused survey found about 43% have no major issue buying games with AI disclosures, 26% are neutral and about 31% are negative (same secondary source). Sample and wording differences likely explain the gap.
- GDC 2026 State of the Game Industry (Jan 2026): 52% of game industry professionals think gen AI is having a negative impact on the industry — [BusinessWire / GDC, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260129438528/en/2026-State-of-the-Game-Industry-Report-Reveals-Widening-Effect-of-Layoffs-Broader-Perspectives-on-Generative-AI-Unionization-Tariffs-and-More)

**AI-native games: state of play**
- 2026 analysis: still no breakout AI-native game proving the category. Most AI characters are optional features or experiments — [Frisson Labs, "It's 2026… where are all the AI NPCs?"](https://www.frisson-labs.com/ai-npcs-2026)
- NetEase *Justice* mobile (China): "Dreamweaver Mode" launched 3 Apr 2026 and generates scenes, characters and endings from player text or voice. AI retainers have long-term memory. Reportedly topped mobile popularity charts at release — [NexWorld AI-Native Games Panorama 2025–2026 (wiki; verify)](https://nexworld.wiki/en/ai-games/ai-games-panorama-2026/)
- Giant Network *Paranormal Task Force*: AI Challenge Mode launched Jan 2026, with a reported 25 M+ AI matches within two months — [NexWorld wiki (single source; verify)](https://nexworld.wiki/en/ai-games/ai-games-panorama-2026/)
- Cautionary example: in NetEase's *Where Winds Meet* (PC/console), open-ended NPC chat without strict guardrails became a sandbox for breaking the game — [myhostnews (secondary)](https://myhostnews.com/ai-in-games-from-living-npcs-to-fully-generated-games-where-are-we-really-going-in-2026/)
- Inworld (conversational NPC middleware) raised $50 M at a $500 M valuation. That is an older round — [Synthedia](https://synthedia.substack.com/p/inworld-raises-new-50m-round-hits)
- LLM NPC build and pricing approaches in 2026 — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/aplusgamesstudio/ai-npcs-in-2026-how-llm-characters-are-built-and-priced-1688)

**On-device AI (enabling tech)**
- Qualcomm launched the Snapdragon Game AI SDK at GDC (Mar 2026) for on-device AI NPCs and local AI-driven game experiences — [Qualcomm developer blog](https://www.qualcomm.com/developer/blog/2026/03/snapdragon-game-ai-sdk-launch-on-device-ai-in-gaming)
- Gemini Nano via Android AICore and ML Kit GenAI APIs: inference runs on the user's hardware, so there are no server API fees and the model is not bundled in the APK. It is supported only on recent devices (Pixel 8+, Galaxy S24/S25, some Xiaomi/Motorola/Honor), so a cloud fallback is recommended for older phones — [Stora guide, Apr 2026](https://stora.sh/blog/2026-04-13-on-device-ai-android-app-gemini-nano-guide); [My Android Solutions, Mar 2026](https://www.myandroidsolutions.com/2026/03/22/gemini-nano-on-device-android-ai/); [NineTwoThree on Nano 4](https://www.ninetwothree.co/blog/gemini-nano-4-device-support). Version details (Nano v3 vs Nano 4 / Gemma 4 base) conflict across sources and are unverified against Google docs.

### Inferences
- **Opportunity:** an "AI game master" for party or narrative play (improv, murder mystery, tabletop-style adventures) where AI is the gameplay itself rather than a replacement for art or voice. The Steam data suggest the penalty attaches to AI used for cheap assets. That inference rests only on qualitative review coding and needs testing.
- **Positioning risk:** marketing "AI-powered" to under-25s is likely counter-productive (3–7% positive). Adults 45+ are markedly more open (22%), which matches the older French player base (average age 40).
- **Hybrid architecture:** on-device SLM for flagships (zero marginal cost, privacy, offline), with a capped cloud fallback for other devices. Moderation and guardrails are mandatory (see *Where Winds Meet*).
- Under EU rules, AI-generated content transparency (AI Act transparency obligations) and Steam-style disclosure norms should be anticipated. Not researched in depth here.

### Gaps
- No verified retention or revenue data for any Western AI-native mobile game (AI Dungeon, Character.AI games, Inworld-powered titles) in 2025–2026.
- No data on real per-session cost, battery or thermal impact of on-device LLMs in games, or on the installed base of Gemini-Nano-capable devices.
- Could not read the Quantic Foundry breakdown of which AI uses (NPC dialogue vs art vs voice) players accept.

## 3. Social / party, location-based, AR, camera and voice games

### Takeaway
"Phone-as-controller" party play got a major validation from Netflix's late-2025 pivot from 100+ downloadable mobile games to TV party games joined by QR code. Location-based AR is in decline after its launch peaks (Pokémon GO −36% YoY; Monster Hunter Now −69% in year 2), a warning against capex-heavy geo/AR bets.

### Cited Findings
- Netflix (Oct 2025) launched TV party games under a Games tab. Players join by scanning a QR code with their phone, which becomes the controller. Launch titles: Lego Party!, Boggle Party (up to 8 players), Pictionary: Game Night, Tetris Time Warp, Party Crashers: Fool Your Friends. Availability is limited to select TVs and countries at first — [TechCrunch, 9 Oct 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/09/netflix-is-letting-users-play-games-on-their-tv-screens/); [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2025/10/10/netflix-launches-tv-party-games-via-games-tab/)
- Netflix's four focus areas are kids, mainstream, party and narrative games. This is a pivot from the post-2021 strategy of 100+ separately downloaded mobile games — [Forbes, 13 Nov 2025](https://www.forbes.com/sites/dbloom/2025/11/13/netflix-showcases-new-video-game-strategy-in-time-for-holidays/); [Gamigion](https://www.gamigion.com/netflix-games-in-2025-whats-up/)
- Discord Activities: social games inside voice channels (Poker Night, Putt Party, Chess in the Park, Land-io, Sketch Heads). Older coverage — [Discord blog](https://discord.com/blog/server-activities-games-voice-watch-together); [esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/gaming/discord-launches-new-activities-feature-to-play-games-and-watch-youtube-together/)
- Location-based: Scopely bought Niantic's games business (Pokémon GO, Pikmin Bloom, Monster Hunter Now) for $3.5 B, closing 29 May 2025 — [Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2025/03/12/scopely-niantic-game-business-3-5-billion-acquisition); [Wikipedia: Scopely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopely)
- Pokémon GO: 2024 revenue of $796.6 M, down from more than $1 B/yr at its peak — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/pokemon-go-is-niantics-biggest-draw-but-pikmin-blooms-quietly-picking-up-millions/). Sensor Tower-based analysis shows a −36% YoY continuation of its decline. Scopely says $8 B+ lifetime gross spend and 20 M+ weekly active users — [Naavik, "Dissecting Scopely's Mobile Empire in 2026"](https://naavik.co/digest/dissecting-scopelys-mobile-empire/)
- Monster Hunter Now: $219.7 M in year 1, $67.3 M in year 2 (−69%), $49.9 M in year 3 (−26%), $336.9 M total over three years. Store-only figures that exclude its web shop. Earlier estimate for year 2: $63.4 M — [PocketGamer.biz 3-year](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/monster-hunter-now-makes-3369m-in-three-years-on-mobile/); [PocketGamer.biz 2nd anniversary](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/monster-hunter-now-celebrates-second-anniversary-and-271m-in-lifetime-player-spending/)
- Naavik: three Scopely titles generate close to 95% of monthly IAP revenue, and Monster Hunter Now and Pikmin Bloom are run without significant new investment — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/dissecting-scopelys-mobile-empire/)
- France: social games make up 12% of smartphone play — [SELL, Sept 2025](https://www.sell.fr/sites/default/files/essentiel-jeu-video/ejv_septembre_2025_def_0.pdf)

### Inferences
- **White space:** a phone-only, no-TV-required local party game, where everyone uses their own phone in the same room (pass-and-play or join by QR/link with no install). It could combine Jackbox/Netflix-style accessibility with the low friction of web delivery, and an AI host/game master could generate prompts, judge answers and adapt to the group. Netflix's pivot shows big-company belief in phone-as-controller party play. Its TV-only, limited-country rollout leaves the "no TV / on the go" occasion (bars, travel, family dinners) open.
- Location-based AR has a steep decay curve unless it rides a mega-IP. A lightweight "light-geo" layer (e.g., weekly local events) is lower risk than a full AR world.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 data found on Jackbox sales, camera-based or voice-based mobile games, or AR adoption.
- No revenue or engagement data on Netflix party games after launch.

## 4. Business model shifts (web shops/D2C, subscriptions, premium revival, fair F2P)

### Takeaway
Regulation now allows meaningful margin recovery through web shops in the US (anti-steering injunction, Apr 2025, pending Supreme Court review) and in the EU (DMA, with Apple fee structures still applying). Premium is reviving at the margin, while subscriptions and Netflix are moving away from mobile-download catalogues. A new game can be designed web-shop-first and cosmetics/unlock-based from day one.

### Cited Findings
- Epic v. Apple: in April 2025 Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers found Apple in willful violation of the anti-steering injunction. Apple was barred from charging commission on purchases made through external links and from restricting such links. The Supreme Court granted certiorari for the 2026 term — [Wikipedia: Epic Games v. Apple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epic_Games_v._Apple). Apple's SCOTUS brief seeks to vacate the contempt finding; the contested policy was a 27% fee on link-out purchases (12% for small business) — [Tech Insider (secondary)](https://tech-insider.org/apple-scotus-27-percent-app-store-fee-2026/). No Supreme Court decision found as of Oct 2026.
- US developers can now embed "buy on web" links in iOS games with no Apple commission — [Xsolla blog (vendor)](https://xsolla.com/blog/mobile-developers-can-now-link-players-directly-to-web-shops-from-their-games-in-the-us); [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/after-the-epic-vs-apple-ruling-mobile-game-monetization-takes-a-leap-forward/)
- Economics (vendor estimates): about $0.92 net per $1 via a web shop versus $0.70 via the App Store. Claims that top-grossing games make 30–40% of revenue via web shops are vendor marketing — [Game Growth Advisor, May 2026](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-05-05-mobile-game-d2c-web-shop-strategy-2026/). EU DMA alternative terms reportedly stack to an effective 17–20% — [PPMHC article (secondary)](https://ppmhc.org/article/israeli-mobile-game-developers-seize-the-opportunity-in-global-app-store-reforms)
- Store-only trackers miss web-shop revenue (e.g., Monster Hunter Now's web shop), so published revenue figures understate D2C — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/monster-hunter-now-makes-3369m-in-three-years-on-mobile/)
- Premium revival: +77% premium mobile releases in 2025 (about 750). Port revenue grew from about $7 M (2022) to about $15 M (2025). Subscriptions and F2P fatigue are cited as drivers — [GameDev.net](https://gamedev.net/news/premium-mobile-games-are-back-with-releases-up-77-in-2025-r4367/)
- Netflix pivoted from 100+ downloadable mobile games to TV party games, kids, mainstream and narrative — [Forbes, Nov 2025](https://www.forbes.com/sites/dbloom/2025/11/13/netflix-showcases-new-video-game-strategy-in-time-for-holidays/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/09/netflix-is-letting-users-play-games-on-their-tv-screens/)
- Ads still convert. 46% of players say they often buy in-game after ads (Bain 2025) — [Bain](https://www.bain.com/insights/gamer-survey-great-gameplay-is-no-longer-enough-gaming-report-2025/). Zynga reports in-game ads drive 27% of mobile gamers to purchase a new product — [Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/in-game-ads-drive-27-percent-mobile-gamers-to-purchase-new-product-zynga/744293/)

### Inferences
- A viable "fair" model for a new title: a free demo or limited free tier, a one-time premium unlock (Balatro-style $5–10), optional cosmetics, and a web shop to keep about 92% of revenue in the US and recover margin in the EU. Optional rewarded ads only, never interstitials (see the churn data in section 1).
- Legal risk: the US link-out regime depends on the pending SCOTUS outcome, so web-shop economics should not be the only pillar of the business plan.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 data found on Apple Arcade or Google Play Pass subscriber numbers or developer payouts.
- No independent (non-vendor) benchmark of web-shop conversion or share of revenue.
- Did not find specifics on how French or EU players adopt alternative payments or alternative app stores under the DMA.

## 5. Distribution: instant/web games, YouTube Playables, Telegram, Discord, cloud

### Takeaway
Distribution channels that bypass the stores are maturing: YouTube Playables (HTML5 inside YouTube, ad-monetised, 50+ markets), Discord Activities (in-voice-call social games) and Telegram Mini Apps (crypto-heavy). These suit low-friction, social, short-session designs. Hard player or revenue data are scarce.

### Cited Findings
- YouTube Playables: launched as a Premium-only beta in late 2023, later extended to all users on mobile and desktop, with 75+ lightweight games at the time — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/social-media/youtube-gets-into-gaming-with-playables-75-games-you-can-try-right-now); [PhoneArena](https://www.phonearena.com/news/youtube-playables-expands-to-more-users_id158792)
- 2026: HTML5/WebGL/Canvas games inside the YouTube app, monetised via AdSense. ## 6. Recent breakout successes and notable failures 2024–2026

### Takeaway
On mobile, the documented successes are premium, short-session roguelites with a PC halo (*Balatro*) and live-ops 4X giants. The documented failures are post-launch decay of high-cost location/IP games (Monster Hunter Now, Pokémon GO) and Netflix's retreat from a large downloadable mobile catalogue. Big indie breakouts in 2025 (*R.E.P.O.*, *Peak*) were PC co-op social games, which points to a social co-op gap on mobile.

### Cited Findings
- Success: *Balatro* mobile reached about $21.3 M and 3.1 M downloads (AppMagic, via GameDev.net) with a premium, no-IAP, no-ads model — [GameDev.net](https://gamedev.net/news/premium-mobile-games-are-back-with-releases-up-77-in-2025-r4367/); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/balatro-nears-44m-on-mobile-amid-a-sudden-spending-surge/)
- Success (F2P): *Last War: Survival* and *Whiteout Survival* are the top mobile 4X earners, made by Chinese teams — [dev.to (secondary)](https://dev.to/linou518/2026-game-market-mobile-is-maturing-pc-indie-games-are-the-real-opportunity-4hkl)
- PC breakouts *R.E.P.O.* and *Peak* (social co-op), with no mobile equivalent cited — [dev.to](https://dev.to/linou518/2026-game-market-mobile-is-maturing-pc-indie-games-are-the-real-opportunity-4hkl)
- AI-native, China: *Justice* mobile Dreamweaver Mode (Apr 2026) reportedly topped popularity charts, and *Paranormal Task Force* reported 25 M+ AI matches in two months (unverified wiki) — [NexWorld](https://nexworld.wiki/en/ai-games/ai-games-panorama-2026/)
- Decline: Monster Hunter Now −69% in year 2 and −26% in year 3. Pokémon GO is down from more than $1 B/yr to $796.6 M (2024) and continues at −36% YoY — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/monster-hunter-now-makes-3369m-in-three-years-on-mobile/); [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/dissecting-scopelys-mobile-empire/)
- Strategic retreat: Netflix moved away from 100+ downloadable mobile games toward TV party games — [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/dbloom/2025/11/13/netflix-showcases-new-video-game-strategy-in-time-for-holidays/)
- Awards (critical, not commercial): SILT, Backpack Battles, Gambonanza, TOEM, Sea of Stars, Lost Twins 2 and others — [GamingonPhone Awards 2026](https://gamingonphone.com/awards/gpa-2026/indie-mobile-game-of-the-year/)
- Ball x Pit (roguelike ball-breaker) had its first major update on 26 Jan 2026. Mobile availability not confirmed — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ball_x_Pit)

### Inferences
- Patterns of success: short, replayable sessions with deep systems, a fair price, word-of-mouth or streamer virality, and cross-platform halo.
- Patterns of failure: content-hungry live-ops without new investment, novelty-driven AR, and catalogue strategies without a social hook.
- **Synthesised design brief (inference):** a web-first, install-free social party/co-op game for the same room or remote play, with an AI game master that generates content, 10–20 minute sessions, a premium unlock or one-time "party pack" with cosmetics, no interstitials, a web shop, distribution via YouTube Playables, Discord and a link, and a French/European adult (35+) casual-social audience as the beachhead.
