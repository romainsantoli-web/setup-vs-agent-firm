## 1. Misleading ads (fake gameplay): how widespread it is and what regulators have done

### Takeaway
Fake or unrepresentative gameplay ads are still a major, highly visible complaint in 2025-2026. Last War: Survival, one of the highest-grossing mobile games of 2025, is the emblematic case. The UK ASA has a consistent line of precedent: disclaimers such as "Not all images represent actual gameplay" do not cure misleading footage (Playrix 2020, Evony 2024). In late 2025 the ASA's enforcement shifted toward loot-box disclosure in ads (Hero Wars, Marvel Contest of Champions, F1 Clash).

### Cited Findings
- **Last War: Survival** (FirstFun) is described by RTBF (Sept 2025) as "le jeu mobile le plus lucratif de 2025" and as a game that "trompe les consommateurs dans ses publicités". RTBF reports that Last War paid celebrities in many countries to film ads claiming the game is "exactement comme la publicité", which "n'était à nouveau pas le cas". RTBF cites a Sensor Tower estimate of €69.8bn in sector revenue for the year. Source: [RTBF](https://www.rtbf.be/article/le-jeu-mobile-le-plus-lucratif-de-2025-trompe-les-consommateurs-dans-ses-publicites-11666359) (summarized from search results; I could not fetch the page directly).
- French YouTuber **Aywen** says these ads "montrent les côtés les plus attirants d'un jeu et omettent tout ce qui est moins vendeur", so many users are disappointed after installing. She made a parody game, "Not A Fake Game", to test whether the model pays. Source: [RTBF](https://www.rtbf.be/article/un-youtubeur-a-cree-le-jeu-smartphone-des-fausses-pubs-pour-voir-si-ca-rapporte-de-l-argent-11603769)
- Player reviews of Last War on JustUseApp: "the previews and ads of this game are completely FAKE". Another reviewer was surprised it was "a war game to fight other cities/bases and not just zombies". Source: [JustUseApp - Last War reviews](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/6448786147/last-war-survival/reviews)
- A fan-run archive estimates the door-choosing runner minigame shown in Last War ads at "<5%" of actual gameplay; the game is in fact a 4X strategy game. Source: [Fake Ad Games archive](https://fakeadgames.com/archive/last-war-survival). A German outlet says ad and game "have practically nothing in common". Source: [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Someone-actually-made-one-of-those-fake-ad-games-and-it-s-pretty-good.1188256.0.html)
- Wikipedia reports that a Q4 2024 Last War campaign leaned into the "misleading ads" controversy and drew 12.5M+ downloads. This is unverified secondary information. Source: [Wikipedia - Last War: Survival Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Last_War:_Survival_Game)
- **ASA vs Evony: The King's Return (Top Games, 2024):** the ad showed puzzle-solving, but the core game is city-building. The ASA found it misleading under CAP Code rule 3.1. Sources: [Mishcon de Reya](https://www.mishcon.com/news/asa-rules-mobile-game-ad-was-misleading-gameplay-depicted-did-not-reflect-the-core-game-playing-experience); [Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=08831e2c-dd9d-4499-8e16-78de90f2b7a7)
- The ASA's own guidance says small print such as "Not all images represent actual gameplay" is not enough to contradict an ad's overall impression. Source: [ASA - "Don't hate the player, hate the game(play)"](https://www.asa.org.uk/news/don-t-hate-the-player-hate-the-game-play-misleading-gameplay-in-videogame-ads.html) (summarized from search results)
- **[OLDER, 2020] ASA bans Playrix Homescapes and Gardenscapes "pull the pin" ads.** The ASA found the games "in large part consisted of a storyline about renovating a cartoon house or garden and 'match-three' style puzzles". Even with a disclaimer, players would have to play a significant amount of different-style content to reach the advertised gameplay. Playrix argued that the pin puzzles existed as mini-games, and said only 45% of players reach level 20 and 18% reach level 100. Sources: [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/asa-bans-gardenscapes-and-homescapes-misleading-ads); [Marketech APAC](https://marketech-apac.com/homescapes-gardenscapes-ads-banned-by-asa-for-misrepresentation/); [FKKS Advertising Law](https://advertisinglaw.fkks.com/post/102gij0/liar-liar-mobile-game-ads-under-fire-asa-bans-misleading-ads-for-mobile-games)
- **ASA, November 2025:** three rulings on loot-box disclosure in ads, against Hutch Games (*F1 Clash*), Kabam (*Marvel Contest of Champions*) and Nexters (*Hero Wars: Alliance*). A generic "Offers In-App Purchases" label was not enough to signal loot boxes, and requiring a tap on "more" to see the information was not sufficiently clear. Source: [Lexology - ASA monthly insights Nov 2025](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=88e823dd-2544-49a1-8d3b-ca4d0595c924)
- An ASA monitoring sweep (March 2025) found most mobile game ads responsible, but some stereotyped "women as sexual objects". Source: [Marketing Beat](https://www.marketing-beat.co.uk/2025/03/20/asa-women-advertising/). GameHer (April 2025, French) criticizes misleading mobile ads that use "sexisme banalisé" and calls for stronger moderation by the stores. Source: [GameHer](https://gameher.fr/blog/quand-les-pubs-des-jeux-mobiles-ont-recours-au-sexisme-banalise)
- A Malwarebytes post (June 2026) reports fake "virus alert" ads appearing inside mobile games, a related ad-quality problem. Source: [Malwarebytes](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/mobile/2026/06/fake-virus-alerts-are-invading-mobile-games)

### Inferences
- The fake-ad pattern persists because it works commercially: Last War tops the grossing charts despite open ridicule. The UK precedent (disclaimers don't save you) has had limited deterrent effect on global UA practice.
- For a new game, "what you see in the ad is the game" is a credible differentiator and a marketing hook. Players and creators such as Aywen actively mock the gap.

### Gaps
- I found no 2025-2026 ASA ruling specifically on gameplay misrepresentation (the latest found is Evony, 2024). The ASA rulings database should be checked directly.
- I found no French regulator (DGCCRF/ARPP) action on fake mobile game ads in 2025-2026.
- No 2025 ruling was found for Royal Kingdom, Hero Wars gameplay ads (as opposed to loot-box disclosure) or Last War.

## 2. Grind, time-gating, FOMO, daily chores and burnout

### Takeaway
Players describe late-game gacha and live-service play as "doing dailies", with login rewards driving FOMO rather than fun. However, I found little hard 2025-2026 data quantifying burnout. The evidence below is mostly anecdotal and academic.

### Cited Findings
- A Girls' Frontline 2: Exilium reviewer says progression is gated behind daily tasks and gacha, and that after a few months the only thing left is "doing dailies 10 minutes every day". Source: [Steam discussion - GFL2](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3308670/discussions/0/592888815005212663)
- A Throne and Liberty player says login rewards create "mad FOMO". Another player counters that people come back because they want to play, not because of the rewards. Source: [Steam discussion - Throne and Liberty](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2429640/discussions/0/4693405245806829936)
- A 2025 MDPI paper on gacha addiction (focused on Genshin Impact) notes these games are "increasingly popular yet controversial due to their addictive mechanics, often likened to gambling". Source: [MDPI Information 16(10):890](https://www.mdpi.com/2078-2489/16/10/890)
- A CDiGRA 2025 content analysis of the **top 50 grossing F2P games on Google Play US (as of 16 June 2025)** found that all of them contain dark-pattern design elements. Daily rewards, opaque virtual currencies and loot boxes were common, and mobile titles deployed them more densely and aggressively than console games. Source: [DiGRA library](https://dl.digra.org/index.php/dl/article/download/3101/3085/3144) (from search summary)

### Inferences
- "Dailies as chores" and FOMO-gated events are a structural feature of top-grossing F2P, not an edge case. A design that is "play when you want, no punishment for absence" addresses a clear pain point.

### Gaps
- I found no quantitative 2025-2026 survey on mobile burnout, daily-login fatigue or churn caused by FOMO events.
- I found no strong sourced data on power creep complaints, though this is a commonly cited theme.

## 3. Repetitiveness, shallow content and reskin or clone fatigue

### Takeaway
The clearest evidence is indirect: a single hybrid-casual hook (door runner, pin pull) is used to sell an unrelated 4X or match-3 core. The JustUseApp and Common Sense reviews below describe content that becomes a monetization wall rather than offering depth.

### Cited Findings
- The Last War ads versus core game gap (runner minigame "<5%" of gameplay) shows a broader pattern: many ads are reskins of the same few hooks. Source: [Fake Ad Games archive](https://fakeadgames.com/archive/last-war-survival)
- A Common Sense Media parent review of Royal Match: "after it hooks the player in with a number of simple levels, the levels then become quite difficult to beat. The temptation is then to spend money on the power-ups and boosters." Source: [Common Sense Media - Royal Match parent reviews](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/royal-match/user-reviews/adult)
- A Supercell spinoff with weak depth and retention: Supercell said Squad Busters "did not meet the high expectations" of players and Supercell. Even after the "Heroes" overhaul (the "boldest game change of this scale" in its live-game history), it was "not able to find a lasting solution to its core problems". In-app purchases ended 30 Oct 2025. Source: [Supercell - Squad development ending](https://supercell.com/en/news/squad-development-ending/); [Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2025/10/30/we-were-wrong-supercell-to-shut-down-squad-busters-two-years-after-games-release)

### Inferences
- Clone fatigue is visible in the number of fake-ad hooks. Players increasingly say they want the game that the ad shows; the "Not A Fake Game" parody (Aywen) and fan archives of fake-ad games show there is latent demand for those simple hooks as real games.

### Gaps
- I found no Reddit or press source with quantified "same game reskinned" sentiment for 2025-2026. This needs direct Reddit/Google Play review mining.

## 4. Rigged difficulty in puzzle games (booster walls, near-miss)

### Takeaway
Royal Match (Dream Games) is the most visible example. Players and parents say difficulty spikes are designed to push boosters and coins, while the developer states that every level is beatable without boosters. The evidence is anecdotal; "rigged" is not proven.

### Cited Findings
- Dream Games' help center: "all levels in the game are beatable without using boosters or spending coins". Source: [Royal Match Help Center](https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/faq/9-level-is-too-hard-1661775375/)
- Common Sense Media parent reviewers call Royal Match a "money-grabbing scheme" where advancing is "virtually impossible without spending money". Source: [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/royal-match/user-reviews/adult)
- A Sikayetvar thread is titled "Royal Match feels rigged after level 500". One user reports using "over 50 lives just trying to pass a single level". Source: [Sikayetvar/Xolvie](https://www.sikayetvar.com/en/royal-match-us/royal-match-feels-rigged-after-level-500-what-strategies-actually-help-you-progress-fairly-q-33661)
- JustUseApp reviews of Royal Match: "after about level 2300 the game turned into pay to win". Others say "the levels are randomly filled so you are not evenly matched" and that "the power ups don't work as they should". Source: [JustUseApp - Royal Match](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1482155847/royal-match/reviews)
- A Medium essay, "The Con Game That Is Royal Match", is an opinion piece that is highly critical of the game's design. Source: [Medium - lasuzanne](https://lasuzanne.medium.com/the-con-game-that-is-royal-match-8549929fa771)
- Many search results for Royal Match were spam "coin generator / cheats" pages. This suggests strong frustration-driven demand for workarounds, but those pages are scams and are not cited as evidence.

### Inferences
- Transparent difficulty (shown or guaranteed solvable seeds, no hidden dynamic difficulty, generous undo) could directly answer the "rigged" perception in match-3 and puzzle.

### Gaps
- I found no academic or regulatory finding that confirms dynamic difficulty manipulation or engineered near-misses in specific 2025 puzzle games. No data was found for Royal Kingdom specifically.

## 5. Dark patterns: manipulative design, loss aversion, artificial scarcity and studies

### Takeaway
Academic work from 2024-2025 shows dark patterns are near-universal in top-grossing mobile F2P. Players rate monetary dark patterns (pay walls, loot boxes) as the most harmful. Consumer groups (NCC "Insert Coin", BEUC) and the US FTC (Genshin, $20M, Jan 2025) have targeted loot boxes and hidden costs.

### Cited Findings
- **CDiGRA 2025 (Chen, Wang, Xiao):** all of the top-50 grossing F2P games on Google Play US (June 2025) contain dark patterns, with "denser and more aggressive deployment" than console games. Source: [DiGRA](https://dl.digra.org/index.php/dl/article/download/3101/3085/3144)
- **Niknejad et al. (ACM MUM 2024), "Level Up or Game Over"**: analysis of user-generated data on 1,496 mobile games found more than 50k dark-pattern instances, including in games "that may seem benign". Source: [ACM DL](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3701571.3701604); [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/386555504_Level_Up_or_Game_Over_Exploring_How_Dark_Patterns_Shape_Mobile_Games)
- **Veiga et al. (ICEIS 2025), "Dark Patterns in Games: An Empirical Study of Their Harmfulness"**: in a survey of 30 players, Pay Wall and Loot Boxes were perceived as the most harmful. Grinding and endowed progress were seen as less harmful. Source: [SciTePress PDF](https://www.scitepress.org/publishedPapers/2025/133658/pdf/index.html)
- **[OLDER, May 2022] Norwegian Consumer Council, "Insert Coin"**: describes loot boxes as "mystery packages" bought with real money. It denounces "exploiting consumers through predatory mechanisms, fostering addiction, targeting vulnerable consumer groups" (Finn Myrstad), layered virtual currencies that obscure real costs, and targeting of minors, and names FIFA and Raid: Shadow Legends as examples. More than 20 consumer groups in 18 countries backed the report and demanded "a ban on deceptive design, extra protections for minors, and transactional transparency". Sources: [Forbrukerrådet](https://www.forbrukerradet.no/siste-nytt/loot-boxes-how-the-gaming-industry-manipulates-and-exploits-consumers/); [BEUC](https://www.beuc.eu/news/loot-boxes-how-gaming-industry-manipulates-and-exploits-consumers); [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/18-european-country-groups-have-joined-the-fight-against-loot-boxes)
- **FTC v. Cognosphere (HoYoverse, Genshin Impact), January 2025:** a $20M settlement. The FTC alleged the company "unfairly marketed loot boxes to children that obscured real costs and misled all players about the odds" and violated COPPA. Remedies: no loot-box sales to under-16s without parental consent, disclosure of odds and exchange rates, and an option to buy loot boxes directly with USD. HoYoverse disputed the claims. Sources: [FTC business blog](https://ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2025/01/level-tips-businesses-ftcs-settlement-genshin-impact-developer-hoyoverse); [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/gaming/ftc-cracks-down-on-genshin-impact-gacha-loot-box-practices). The status of court approval is conflicting across sources ([MyChesCo](https://www.mychesco.com/a/news/national/ftc-finalizes-20-million-settlement-over-genshin-impact-loot-box-practices/) says "finalizes" in its headline but "awaits approval" in its body).
- **Pokémon TCG Pocket trading (early 2025):** the trading system required Trade Tokens, earned mainly by burning rare cards; a 2-star card gave only 300 tokens. Players called it confusing and tedious and some threatened to quit. The developers cited anti-bot and anti-multi-account reasons, critics saw it as a monetization lever, and the developers later announced replacing Trade Tokens with shinedust. Sources: [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/pokemon-tcg-pockets-trading-feature-is-changing-after-fan-backlash); [Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/pokemon-tcg-pocket/trade-update); [The Outer Haven](https://www.theouterhaven.net/2025/02/pokemon-tcg-pocket-backlash-leads-to-shifts-in-trading-feature/)
- Last War reviewer (monetization): one claims the game's rewards penalize big spenders relative to low spenders. This is a single, unverified review. Source: [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/6448786147/last-war-survival/reviews)

### Inferences
- The Pokémon TCG Pocket case is instructive. A broadly praised game (no ads, generous free packs) took heavy reputational damage from one friction-by-design social feature. Players notice and punish friction that looks like monetization even in "fair" games.

### Gaps
- I could not verify the current (2026) state of the Pokémon TCG Pocket trading changes or the final court approval of the FTC/HoYoverse order.
- I did not fetch the Dark Pattern Games database (darkpattern.games) directly.

### Takeaway
The evidence I found here is thin. The main data point is Supercell's Squad Busters, a social and party-oriented game that failed and is being shut down. I found no 2025-2026 study on loneliness or the lack of real friend play in mobile games.

Sources disagree on the final dates: one says the final update was planned for Dec 2025, another says the game stops working 14 Dec 2026. ### Inferences
- There appears to be a gap for genuinely cooperative, friend-first or local/party mobile play. This inference is weakly supported here and needs other researchers' data.

### Gaps
- No sourced 2025-2026 data was found on toxicity, a lack of local multiplayer, or loneliness in mobile gaming.

## 7. Children and families

### Takeaway
Regulators and researchers in 2025 focused on children: the FTC's Genshin settlement over loot boxes for minors, and an arXiv study finding ads in 90% of popular children's apps, mostly unskippable and combined with deceptive patterns. Parents' reviews of Royal Match describe pressure to spend.

### Cited Findings
- **Krahl, Hartwig et al. (TU Darmstadt), "Playful but Persuasive" (arXiv, Dec 2025):** studied 20 popular free children's apps in German-speaking regions. Advertising appeared in 90% of them, was mostly non-skippable, and was closely tied to deceptive patterns. The authors describe a "systemic failure of existing safeguards", note parental controls easy for children to circumvent, and warn of longer screen time, weaker self-regulation and accidental purchases. This is a preprint and has not been peer reviewed. Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.17819); [Deceptive Design summary](https://deceptive.design/articles/playful-but-persuasive-deceptive-designs-and-advertising-strategies-in-popular-mobile-apps-for-children); [Digital Child (press write-up)](https://digitalchild.org.au/kids-apps-deceptive-design/)
- FTC/Genshin: the FTC cited the "vibrant anime-style graphics and child-like characters" as appealing to minors, and the settlement requires parental consent for under-16 loot-box purchases. Source: [FTC](https://ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2025/01/level-tips-businesses-ftcs-settlement-genshin-impact-developer-hoyoverse)
- [OLDER 2022] NCC "Insert Coin" lists targeting minors among its key concerns. Source: [Forbrukerrådet](https://www.forbrukerradet.no/siste-nytt/loot-boxes-how-the-gaming-industry-manipulates-and-exploits-consumers/)
- Royal Match parent reviews on Common Sense Media (see section 4). Source: [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/royal-match/user-reviews/adult)

### Inferences
- A kid-safe, family-co-play title with no ads, no loot boxes and a transparent one-time price has regulatory tailwinds (FTC, EU consumer groups) and an explicit parental demand.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 statistic was found on how much minors spend on mobile games, and no French data was found (e.g. CNIL, Arcom, UFC-Que Choisir).

## 8. Story and meaning: shallow narratives and shutdowns erasing purchases

### Takeaway
End of service is a recurring, concrete grievance. In 2025-2026 many gacha titles shut down with purchases disabled, data deleted and limited or no refunds. One Japanese analysis says more than 70% of gacha and live-service games die before year three. Even major brands are shutting down mobile titles (Mario Kart Tour, Sept 2026; FFVII Ever Crisis, Oct 2026).

### Cited Findings
- **Soul Tide** (ended 28 Feb 2025): "all player data will be deleted", so unspent currency and characters were lost. Source: [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/soul-tide/end-of-service-announcement)
- **Gran Saga** (ended 30 Apr 2025): refunds possible until 30 May, but "may not be possible if you've already used your purchases". Source: [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/gran-saga/end-of-service-announcement)
- **KonoSuba: Fantastic Days** (ended 30 Jan 2025): purchases stopped Oct 2024, with a refund window. Source: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/16494-konosuba-fantastic-days-shuts-down-simultaneously-worldwide-on-january-30-2025)
- **Final Fantasy Brave Exvius (Japan)** ended 31 Oct 2025, after nearly 10 years. Source: [KitGuru](https://www.kitguru.net/lifestyle/mobile/mustafa-mahmoud/final-fantasy-brave-exvius-is-fully-shutting-down-this-october/)
- **FFVII Ever Crisis** ends 6 Oct 2026, with refunds for unused currency only for Taiwan residents. **Mario Kart Tour** ends 29 Sept 2026. Source: search summary of [Berkeley Historical Society aggregator](https://berkeleyhistoricalsociety.org/square-enix-and-nintendo-end-service-today-mario-kart-and-more-announced/). This is a low-quality aggregator and the dates should be verified with official sources.
- **More than 70% of Japanese gacha/live-service games fail before year 3.** This comes from a single analysis and is indicative only. Source: [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/japans-gacha-game-bubble-bursts-as-most-titles-die-within-3-years)
- **Squad Busters** (Supercell) shut down about 1.5-2 years after launch (see section 6). Source: [Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2025/10/30/we-were-wrong-supercell-to-shut-down-squad-busters-two-years-after-games-release)

### Inferences
- "Offline-playable, buy-once, yours forever" (or a guaranteed offline end-of-service patch) is a concrete trust promise a new game can make against the shutdown risk.

### Gaps
- No sourced quotes were found on shallow narratives or abrupt story endings in 2025-2026 mobile games.
- The Stop Killing Games (EU citizens' initiative) movement as it applies to mobile was not researched.

## 9. What players want but can't find, and counter-examples praised for respecting players

### Takeaway
Premium or subscription titles with no ads and no IAP (Balatro, Apple Arcade and Play Pass catalogs, Monument Valley, Mini Metro, Bloons TD 6) are repeatedly cited as what players seek. Balatro mobile is the standout 2024-2025 example: a 5.0 App Store rating, about $4.2M net mobile revenue (AppMagic estimate), and explicit praise for "no microtransactions, no battle passes".

### Cited Findings
- **Balatro** launched on iOS and Android on 26 Sept 2024 at $9.99, and as **Balatro+** on Apple Arcade, where there are "no ads or in-app purchases". AppMagic estimated more than $4.2M net mobile revenue. One article reported a 5.0 App Store rating from about 8,000 reviews (late 2024). Reddit commenters praised the game's lack of microtransactions and battle passes. Sources: [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/apple-arcade-gets-eight-games-this-month-including-balatro/); [Yahoo/Balatro mobile](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/balatro-mobile-release-date-poker-162131892.html); [Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/save-your-wallet-and-time-with-this-goty-contender-that-has-no-microtransactions-or-battle-passes); [WN Hub - 3.5M copies](https://wnhub.io/news/finance/item-46507). These are late-2024 figures and may have changed.
- Listicles that recommend ad-free games cite Mini Metro (premium, ad-free), Minecraft (one-time fee), Monument Valley (skill-based, no P2W), Among Us (free, ad-free) and Bloons TD 6 (IAP but "entirely playable without ads"). They point to Apple Arcade and Google Play Pass as curated ad-free catalogs. Sources are low quality: [esports.net](https://www.esports.net/news/mobile-games/best-mobile-games-without-ads/); [Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/top-games/mobile-games-no-ads)
- Demand signal: Aywen's parody "Not A Fake Game" was made to test whether a real version of the fake-ad gameplay would sell. Source: [RTBF](https://www.rtbf.be/article/un-youtubeur-a-cree-le-jeu-smartphone-des-fausses-pubs-pour-voir-si-ca-rapporte-de-l-argent-11603769). Notebookcheck says another fake-ad-style game someone built is "pretty good". Source: [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Someone-actually-made-one-of-those-fake-ad-games-and-it-s-pretty-good.1188256.0.html)
- Pokémon TCG Pocket's positives (no ads, free daily packs) were offset by the trading backlash (see section 5).

### Inferences
- Combined player wishlist from the evidence: honest ads, no forced ads, a one-time price or a transparent cosmetic-only model, fair and solvable difficulty, play-on-your-schedule (no punitive dailies), offline or owned content, and kid-safe defaults.
- The fake-ad hooks themselves (pin-pull, door-runner) are a validated appeal. A game that makes the advertised gameplay its real core, with depth, fills a gap that players openly joke about.

### Gaps
- Direct Reddit threads from r/MobileGaming, r/AndroidGaming and r/iosgaming (2025-2026) did not surface through search. Quotes from those threads need direct retrieval.
- No 2025-2026 data was found on Netflix Games' or Apple Arcade's player satisfaction, or on Vampire Survivors, Stardew Valley or Slay the Spire mobile reception.
- French-language Google Play 1-2 star reviews were not retrieved. The only French quotes found come from RTBF and GameHer coverage.
