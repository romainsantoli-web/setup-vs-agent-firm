## Q1. Which mobile games top the charts by revenue and downloads in 2025-2026 (global, France/Europe, Google Play)?

### Inferences
- The 2026 viral pattern is short-lived hybrid-casual puzzle clones (bus jam, arrow, block) that spike through ad-driven user acquisition and are then displaced by near-identical copies. These games are likely sources of ad-related negative reviews.
- The 2025-2026 revenue risers are long-tail live-ops games (4X, merge) that grow through heavy user acquisition and deep IAP. These are likely sources of pay-to-win and monetization complaints.

## Q3. What are the dominant genres and business models, and how is the market split?

### Takeaway
Free-to-play with in-app purchases is close to universal: **94% of French mobile game revenue comes from IAP** (SELL). Revenue is concentrated in **4X strategy, puzzle/merge, casino-like casual (Monopoly GO!, Coin Master), MOBA/battle royale and UGC (Roblox)**. Strategy was the only category growing in revenue, downloads and time spent in 2025. Hybrid-casual is the only casual segment growing in IAP. Ads matter most for puzzle and hybrid-casual games.

### Cited Findings
- **France 2025 (SELL Bilan, April 2026):** total market €5.86B (+3%). **Mobile €1.792B (+11%), about 30% of the market**, the fastest-growing segment. Console €2.55B (44%), PC about €1.51B. **94% of French mobile revenue comes from in-app purchases.** — [SELL EJV Avril 2026 PDF](https://www.sell.fr/sites/default/files/essentiel-jeu-video/ejv_avril_2026_final_.pdf); [AFJV](https://afjv.com/news/11964_essentiel-marche-francais-jeu-video-sell.htm); [Level-1](https://level-1.fr/le-jeu-video-en-france-atteint-58-milliards-deuros-en-2025/)

- For a French Android audience, the business models a review study should expect are: (a) heavy-IAP 4X and merge games with pay-to-win and spending-pressure complaints; (b) ad-dense hybrid-casual puzzle games with forced-ad, fake-ad and ad-frequency complaints; (c) gacha and loot-style packs in Pokémon TCG Pocket and Free Fire with odds and fairness complaints; (d) UGC (Roblox) with moderation and child-safety complaints.

## Q4. What key market trends are reported for 2026?

### Takeaway
2026 is defined by (1) a mature, flat-to-declining store IAP market with **falling downloads**, (2) **web shops / D2C** moving spend off Google Play and the App Store after the Epic rulings, (3) **lower Google Play fees** under the Epic settlement and new **EU DMA terms from Apple**, (4) the growth of **hybrid-casual** and **4X strategy**, and (5) cross-platform launches (PC/console/mobile). No hard data was found on AI-in-games adoption.

### Cited Findings
- **Market maturity:** Sensor Tower's State of Mobile 2026 (as summarised) puts worldwide mobile game IAP at about $40B (-2% YoY; scope probably H2 or a narrower window, so treat with caution), playtime at 221B hours, and downloads at -12% to 24B. Note that this conflicts in scope with the $82B full-year figure in Q1. — [Gamigion](https://www.gamigion.com/state-of-mobile-2026-takeaways-from-sensor-tower-report/); [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-the-state-of-the-mobile)
- Mobile game downloads are at their lowest level since the start of the COVID-19 pandemic (Sensor Tower). — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/analytics/item-50926)
- Store IAP across iOS and Google Play (excluding web shops) fell from about $21B in Q3 2025 to $19.6B in Q3 2026 (-7%). — [tech-insider](https://tech-insider.org/mobile-game-spending-falls-apple-eu-fees-2026) (secondary source; the decline is partly attributed to web-shop migration)
- **Web shops:** more than 60 of the top 100 grossing mobile games run a web shop, and D2C game payments grew about 46% YoY in 2025 (vendor estimate). — [GameGrowthAdvisor](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-05-05-mobile-game-d2c-web-shop-strategy-2026/). SciPlay made $53M from D2C in April-June 2026, about 29% of its earnings. — [PocketGamer.biz Top 50 makers 2026](https://pocketgamer.biz/the-top-50-mobile-game-makers-of-2026)
- **US, Epic v. Apple:** no commission is collected on US link-out purchases as of July 2026. The Ninth Circuit (December 2025) remanded for a "reasonable" fee, which is still undetermined, and Apple is seeking Supreme Court review. — [Unity blog](https://unity.com/blog/mobile-game-webshop-business-case); [Mavan](https://www.mavan.com/are-mobile-game-web-shops-a-growth-lever-in-2026/)
- **Google Play after the Epic settlement:** one source reports tiered fees from June 30, 2026 (10% on the first $1M, 20% on new-install revenue, 25% on existing users). Another describes a flat 20% (**conflict**). Google has also announced an external-link fee of up to ~20% that is not yet in effect. — [tech-insider](https://tech-insider.org/epic-games-store-vs-google-play-vs-galaxy-store-2026/); [Mavan](https://www.mavan.com/are-mobile-game-web-shops-a-growth-lever-in-2026/); [Unity](https://unity.com/blog/mobile-game-webshop-business-case); [Wikipedia: Epic v. Google](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epic_Games_v._Google)
- **EU DMA:** Apple's new DMA terms, effective **October 1, 2026**, replace the per-install Core Technology Fee with a flat **5% Core Technology Commission** for apps distributed through alternative marketplaces. — [tech-insider](https://tech-insider.org/mobile-game-spending-falls-apple-eu-fees-2026)

## Q5. Key metrics: downloads, revenue, retention, player demographics

### Takeaway
France has **40.2M players** (2025), an average age of **40**, a near 50/50 gender split, and the **smartphone (59%) as the most-used device**. The French mobile market is worth €1.79B (+11%). Globally, mobile game revenue is about $82B (2025), downloads are about 50B and falling, and monthly store spend is about $6.6B in mid-2026.

### Cited Findings
- **SELL/Médiamétrie "Les Français et le jeu vidéo" (September 2025, about 4,000 people aged 10-80):** 40.2M players (a record), average age 40, 88% adults (35.4M), 49% women (19.5M). Players use 2.2 devices on average: **smartphone 59%**, console 58%. 76% play at least weekly, for about 6h25 per week. Seniors favour the smartphone and casual games. — [SELL EJV septembre 2025 PDF](https://www.sell.fr/sites/default/files/essentiel-jeu-video/ejv_septembre_2025_def_0.pdf); [AFJV](https://afjv.com/news/11790_etude-mediametrie-sell-francais-jeux-video-2025.htm); [Actualités Jeux Vidéo](https://actualitesjeuxvideo.fr/les-francais-et-le-jeu-video-sell-syndicat-des-editeurs-de-logiciels-de-loisirs-402-millions-de-joueurs-en-france-en-2025/). One passage gives smartphone 55% vs console 47% vs PC 40%, probably a different scope, possibly the 4-16 panel (**conflict**).
- **France mobile revenue 2025:** €1.792B, +11%, about 30% of the French games market, 94% from IAP. — [AFJV](https://afjv.com/news/11964_essentiel-marche-francais-jeu-video-sell.htm)
- **Global:** about $82B mobile game revenue in 2025 (+1%) and 50.4B downloads (-7.2%) per Sensor Tower. — [Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/mobile-games-82-billion-revenue-2025-sensor-tower-report); [GameWorldObserver](https://gameworldobserver.com/2026/01/21/in-2025-the-revenue-of-the-mobile-gaming-market-grew-but-only-slightly-analysis)
- **Monthly, August 2026:** $6.57B spend and 3.76B downloads (App Store + Google Play). — [Sensor Tower Aug 2026](https://sensortower.com/blog/top-10-worldwide-mobile-games-by-revenue-and-downloads-in-august-2026)
- **Gossip Harbor engagement:** about 5.4M DAU and 28M MAU (AppMagic). — [Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/gossip-harbor)
- **Retention:** hybrid-casual D7 now beats the top 25 casual games, and casual D7 has been declining since 2022 (Sensor Tower). — [GameDev Reports, State of Gaming 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)

### Inferences
- The typical French mobile player is an adult (average age 40), with near gender parity and many seniors on casual games. This matters for review analysis: complaints about ads and difficulty walls in puzzle and merge games will come from a broad adult audience, while Roblox, Free Fire and Brawl Stars complaints skew younger.

### Gaps
- No per-game D1/D7/D30 retention figures were found for specific titles.
- No per-game French download figures were found.
